How to read a row

Size

Rough judgement for one developer — S days, M one to two weeks, L three weeks or more. Not an estimate; the SoW sets dates after estimation against the FSDs.

Wave

When it can start, based on what it depends on — W0 first through W5 last. Modules run in parallel, so the wave is what keeps order straight across them.

Depends

Must land first. A1–A8 are SoW assumptions, all externally owned and none resolved. D-1–D-9 are the decisions listed at the end.

Modules

Eleven groups: the cross-cutting foundations, then the ten SoW modules (a) through (j).

Out of scope for v1 — do not build, do not stub

Automated handwritten Urdu/Modi transcription as trusted data · silent promotion of links into the verified layer · GIS and geo-mapping · enterprise search over chat and email bodies · citizen-facing portals · OneDrive and S3/GCS connectors · graph-explorer UI · graph analytics · schema designer · a separate graph database · DPDP consent and erasure workflows · cleaning rules engine · clustering UI · scheduled reports · custom report builder · cross-document synthesis · saved queries · additional Indic scripts · template auto-suggestion · active learning.

Anything marked P1 or P2 in the PRD enters v1 only through a signed change request under §10. The SoW singles out “small” connector additions as the historically likeliest scope-creep vector.

Sizes are judgement, not estimates. They come from reading the code, and express relative weight only. The SoW sets dates after estimation against the FSDs.

Authority. The SoW names the PRD v1.0 as the requirements authority. Where this backlog and the PRD disagree, §1 says escalate to the product owner rather than resolve it silently.
